## 1. Method and data quality

**Primary source — Hugging Face Hub API, pulled 13 Nov 2026.** 5,822 unique model repos across `text-to-image`, `image-to-image`, `text-to-video`, `image-to-video`, `image-text-to-video`, `video-to-video`, `any-to-any` (top 1,000 by downloads per tag). `downloads` = **last 30 days**, which is what we want for *current* demand. Total tracked: **123.8 M downloads/30d**.

**Secondary source — Civitai API**, top 600 models sorted by *Most Downloaded / Month*, deduplicated to 1,396 model versions. Civitai's per-version `downloadCount` is **all-time**, so we use it as (a) an ecosystem-size signal and (b) a "currently trending" filter, not as a monthly number. Civitai matters because the SDXL/anime/H3 crowd lives there and barely touches HF.

**VAE identity was verified from artefacts, not marketing**: `vae/config.json`, `model_index.json`, and the actual VAE filenames shipped in ComfyUI repacks. Every latent-space claim in §4 is backed by a config or a shipped file.

**Caveats you should keep in mind when reading the numbers**
- HF counts **per-file** downloads, so multi-quant GGUF repos (unsloth, QuantStack, city96) are inflated relative to single-file repos. Directionally fine, absolutely noisy.
- `Comfy-Org/Wan-2.2` (9.74 M) is a mega-repack containing A14B **and** 5B/Fun/Camera models → it straddles the 16 ch and 48 ch spaces. We attribute it to 16 ch (the A14B files dominate the file list); the 48 ch row is therefore a lower bound.
- CI, mirrors and re-upload farms add noise (e.g. `comfyicu/LTX-2.5`, 1.81 M with 15 likes, is almost certainly automated).
- HF **undercounts** the A1111/Forge/Civitai-only world. `Anima` is Civitai-first; SDXL's real footprint is larger than the HF row suggests.
- Download counts ≠ users. Treat them as a *relative* demand proxy, which is exactly what a ranking needs.

## 2. The structural finding that changes your economics

Your constraint is "one model per VAE, ~$200 each". The good news: **VAEs are shared far more than models are.** Five latent spaces cover ~75 % of traffic; eight cover ~94 %.

Verified sharing:

1. **Wan2.1-VAE == Qwen-Image-1 VAE == Krea-2 VAE == Anima VAE.** `Qwen/Qwen-Image`, `Qwen/Qwen-Image-Edit-2511` and `Wan-AI/Wan2.2-I2V-A14B` carry **byte-identical `latents_mean`/`latents_std` arrays** (16 values). Krea-2's install instructions download `vae/qwen_image_vae.safetensors`; `circlestone-labs/Anima` ships `split_files/vae/qwen_image_vae.safetensors` ("this is the Qwen-Image VAE, you might already have it"). The author of the leading Wan upscaler states outright: *"compatible with both Wan and Qwen since they share the same latent space."*
   → **One $200 run serves the #1 video model, the #1 image editor, a top-3 aesthetics model (Krea-2) and the #1 anime model (Anima).**
2. **FLUX.3 uses the FLUX.2 VAE — literally the same file.** `black-forest-labs/flux-3-image-klein-comfy` ships `vae/flux2-vae.safetensors`; the diffusers write-up says "FLUX.3 uses the same VAE architecture as Flux.2". **MOSS-Image, ERNIE-Image and Fibo-2.0 also ship `AutoencoderKLFlux2` / `flux2-vae.safetensors`.**
   → **Your existing FlowUpscaler is already a FLUX.3 / MOSS / ERNIE / Fibo upscaler. That is a free market you have not claimed.**
3. **Z-Image, Chroma, HiDream, Lumina-2/Neta, Boogu-Image all use the FLUX.1 VAE** (`AutoencoderKL`, 16 ch, `scaling_factor 0.3611`, `shift_factor 0.1159`).
4. **MiniMax-H3 image and video share one 24 ch space** (`AutoencoderKLMiniMaxH3` and `MiniMaxH3ImageVAE` have identical latent stats).
5. **The exception that costs you a run: Qwen-Image-2.1 broke away.** New `AutoencoderKLQwenImage21`, **64 ch, 16× spatial, 8× temporal, 4-channel RGBA in/out**. Nothing transfers from Qwen-Image-1. Same for LTX-2.3, which "rebuilt the VAE architecture… a new latent space".

**Strategic implication:** your experiments say cross-VAE training/adaptation is worse than from-scratch. You do **not** need cross-VAE generalisation to get reach — you need ~6 correctly chosen per-VAE models. Pick by *latent space*, not by model brand, and you buy 85 % of the market for $1,200.

## 6. Ranked dossiers

**How the download estimates were built.** `expected ≈ base_traffic × attach_rate × our_share`, with attach_rate 0.5–6 % (fraction of base-model users who need an upscaler at all) and our_share 15–70 % (competition). Calibrated on three observed anchors: the H3 community upscaler at **354 k/mo**, spacepxl's Wan decoder at **16 k/mo**, and your FlowUpscaler at **81/mo**. Treat them as order-of-magnitude, first-6-months averages, assuming competent distribution (§7). Without distribution, divide by ~10.

### #1 — Wan2.1-VAE, 16 ch f8t4 (Wan2.2-A14B · Qwen-Image-1/Edit · Krea-2 · Anima)
- **Why first:** 23.9 % of all tracked traffic, 1,289 repos, and the only space that is simultaneously top-tier in **video** (Wan is *the* local video workhorse; Wan 2.5/2.6 are API-only, so 2.2 will not be displaced soon), **editing** (Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 is still the likeness-preservation favourite), **aesthetics** (Krea-2, 3.96 M Civitai downloads across trending versions) and **anime** (Anima, 2.07 M HF + 613 k Civitai).
- **The pain:** Wan outputs 480p/720p and everyone wants 1080p; Qwen-1/Krea/Anima output ~1 MP and everyone wants 2–4 MP. The current answers are a full second sampling pass, a slow pixel-space SeedVR2 pass, or an image-only decoder trick.
- **Build:** 2× and 4× heads; image-first (cheapest, covers Qwen/Krea/Anima), then temporal-consistent video. Train on mixed photo + anime + text/typography data — the incumbent's explicit weakness is anime/lineart/text, and that is where the Civitai volume is.
- **Estimate: 150 k–500 k/mo.** Risks: Qwen-Image-1 traffic will bleed into 2.1 over 2026–27, but Krea-2 + Anima + Wan keep the space alive; verify the Wan↔Qwen latent identity empirically (encode with one, decode with the other) before shipping a single unified checkpoint.

### #2 — MiniMax-H3 VisualVAE, 24 ch f16t4
- **Why second (and arguably first on pure demand intensity):** 20.3 M/30d, and it is the **only** space where a latent upscaler already does 354 k downloads/month. Base output is 768p short-side by design; the official 2K route is a *second full generation*. Every H3 user is an upscaler user.
- **Bonus:** H3-Image (3 Nov 2026, 306 k in ten days) shares the same 24 ch stats → one run covers video **and** image, plus Pika-H3-Flex, Pruna P-H3-Image and the whole merge ecosystem.
- **Build:** video-half-only upscaling of the joint AV latent; respect `clip_length 17` / `token_drop 3` chunking; 2× (768p→1536p) is the money shot, 4× for stills. The ViT decoder is heavy — measure end-to-end, not just latent time.
- **Estimate: 250 k–700 k/mo.** Risks: MiniMax may ship an official latent upscaler (they already ship the 2K regeneration path); NVIDIA is active here (`Open-H3-Regenerate-2K`, `H3-to-LTX-Latent-Adapter`). Move fast — this window is open *now*.

### #3 — Qwen-Image-2.1 VAE, 64 ch f16t8 RGBA
- **Why third:** the single most-downloaded image model on HF (10.08 M for the Comfy-Org repack alone), a brand-new latent space nobody has covered properly, and a 16× compression that makes decode-side detail loss the dominant complaint.
- **What dampens it:** native 2K output means fewer users *need* an upscaler than for H3; the strong free alternative is "just generate at 2K".
- **Build:** 2× (1K→2K fast path, and 2K→4K), preserve the **alpha channel** (a genuinely differentiating feature — no pixel upscaler does RGBA), and evaluate against both the stock decoder and `texture-fix-vae` (205 k/mo).
- **Estimate: 100 k–350 k/mo.** Risks: Qwen ships native 4K or their own upscaler in 2.2; a future VAE change strands the run (this family already changed VAE once).

### #4 — SDXL-VAE, 4 ch f8 (Illustrious / NoobAI / Pony / RealVisXL / Juggernaut)
- **Why fourth:** 14.4 M HF **plus the largest community on Civitai** (1.61 M downloads across 474 trending versions — Illustrious alone 981 k). Hi-res fix is universal here, so the attach rate is structurally high, and 4-channel latents make this the cheapest, fastest run you will ever do.
- **Build:** 2× + 4×, **anime/lineart-first** data mix, and a hard requirement: match hires-fix quality at ~half the wall-clock. Ship for **A1111/Forge and Civitai**, not only ComfyUI — this audience is not primarily on HF.
- **Estimate: 50 k–200 k/mo on HF, with meaningful Civitai upside.** Risks: "free and good enough" inertia; you win on speed and on not re-rolling the composition.

### #5 — FLUX.1-VAE, 16 ch f8 (Z-Image / Chroma / HiDream / Lumina / Boogu)
- **Why fifth:** 9.3 M/30d and the best growth story in the group — Z-Image-Turbo (6B) is the model people run on 8–12 GB cards, where a second sampling pass hurts most. Pure 2D VAE → simplest training target after SDXL.
- **Build:** 2×/4× image heads; Z-Image + Chroma + FLUX.1 validation set; small enough to bundle in a ComfyUI node.
- **Estimate: 50 k–180 k/mo.** Risks: FLUX.1-dev itself is ageing; the run's value rides on Z-Image and Chroma.

### #6 — FLUX.2/FLUX.3-VAE, 32 ch f8 + 2×2 patch — **do this in week 0, it is nearly free**
- **Why it ranks below its traffic (13.2 M, #5 by volume):** revealed demand is terrible — your FlowUpscaler does **81 downloads/month**. FLUX.2/3 users skew API/pro, FLUX.3 markets *native 4K*, and the model is distributed only via a GitHub README.
- **Why do it anyway:** the asset exists and the space is bigger than you think — **FLUX.3 Image Klein ships the identical `flux2-vae.safetensors`**, and **MOSS-Image, ERNIE-Image and Fibo-2.0** all use `AutoencoderKLFlux2`. One validation pass + a rename gives you four extra model families for ~$0.
- **Actions:** verify FlowUpscaler on FLUX.3/MOSS/ERNIE/Fibo; add a 4× head; re-release as a multi-model "FLUX.2/3 latent upscaler" with ComfyUI Registry listing, Civitai mirror, diffusers example, comparison gallery.
- **Estimate: 20 k–100 k/mo** (i.e. a 250–1000× lift purely from distribution + coverage).

### #7 — SD1.5-VAE, 4 ch f8
5.7 M/30d, still the backbone of AnimateDiff/ControlNet/low-VRAM workflows. Same architecture as SDXL (different scaling) → cheap incremental run, possibly a near-free adaptation of the SDXL model *within the same architecture family* (test it — this is not the cross-VAE case your experiments rejected). **10 k–50 k/mo.**

### #8 — LTX-2.5 / 2.3 latent space
8.3 M combined, but **the provider already ships free, native, ComfyUI-integrated latent upscalers** (spatial ×2, ×1.5, temporal ×2) plus a detailing IC-LoRA. The only compelling angle is collapsing their 3-stage DFR pipeline into one pass. Add the churn risk: LTX-2 → 2.3 → 2.5 in twelve months, with a rebuilt latent space at 2.3. **10 k–50 k/mo**, and only after #1–#6. If you do it: first confirm 2.3↔2.5 latent compatibility so one run covers both.

### #9 — Wan2.2-VAE, 48 ch f16t4 (TI2V-5B)
1.3 M/30d (undercounted — the 5B files also live inside `Comfy-Org/Wan-2.2`), zero existing upscalers, and 5B/TI2V is the low-VRAM video favourite. Cheap win once the Wan pipeline exists. **10 k–40 k/mo.**

### #10+ — LTX-Video 0.9.x (128 ch), Hunyuan, SD3.5, SVD, CogVideoX, SANA, Mochi, Kandinsky
Each ≤ 1.4 M/30d with either first-party upscalers or a shrinking user base. **Skip.** Re-evaluate only if a new release revives one of them.

## 7. The biggest lever is not the model — it is distribution

The same product category, same community, same year:

| Release | Distribution | Downloads / 30d |
|---|---|---|
| LBH-123-AI H3 latent upscaler | HF + ComfyUI custom node + Reddit + ~10 community mirrors | **354,859** |
| spacepxl Wan2.1-VAE-upscale2x | HF + own node pack + slow.pics comparisons | 15,956 |
| **TensorForger/FlowUpscaler** | GitHub README + HF link only | **81** |

That is a ~4,000× gap that has nothing to do with model quality. Concrete checklist for every release:

1. **ComfyUI Registry** listing (not just `git clone`), weights in `ComfyUI/models/latent_upscale_models/` — that folder is now a convention (LTX-2.5 ships its own upscalers there), so a native-feeling node is expected.
2. Get into **comfy.org/hub** and **docs.comfy.org** tutorials; ship a `workflow.json` per base model, named for what people search ("Qwen 2.1 4K", "H3 1080p", "Wan 2.2 upscale").
3. **Civitai mirror** for the SDXL/Illustrious/Anima/H3 crowds (Civitai has an Upscaler model type) + an **A1111/Forge** path for the SD1.5/SDXL base.
4. **diffusers** integration — a `LatentUpscalePipeline`/modular block, following the `LTXLatentUpsamplePipeline` precedent; ask for a HF blog post.
5. Ship **bf16 + fp8 + GGUF**, tiny weights, and a **taesd/taef-style preview decoder** path for ≥4K (you already do this with taef2 — keep it, it is a real differentiator).
6. **Comparison galleries** (slow.pics) against the exact incumbent for that space: hires-fix, SeedVR2, spacepxl, H3-Regenerate-2K. Perceptual proof beats metrics — the spacepxl card explicitly refuses to report PSNR and the community loves it.
7. Post at release in r/StableDiffusion + r/comfyui with a before/after and a wall-clock number.

## 8. Watch list — what would re-order this list

- **Qwen-Image-2.2 with another new VAE** → #3 becomes a moving target; keep the training recipe hot so a re-run is $200, not a research project.

- **FLUX.3 native 4K adoption** → suppresses FLUX.2/3 upscaler demand further.
